# Portfolio and blog

A personal website for Vijayasankar Ravindran, Lead Software Engineer. Visitors
can review his experience and selected projects, open his résumé, contact him,
and read his writing about software engineering, learning, and life.

The portfolio is the homepage. Its approved identity uses factual copy, neutral
light/dark themes, DM Sans, DM Mono for the VNR mark, restrained dividers, and
the approved portrait with its animated gradient. Preserve its content and assets.

The user rejected the formal serif blog and asked for a little playfulness and
minimalism. The writing pages now share the portfolio palette and sans-serif
family. Their distinct character is compact personal notes: modest headings,
rounded tag links, a small geometric blue accent, and a centered reading column.
Keep reading comfortable; no decorative loops, invented posts, or rewritten claims.

A homepage Blog section follows Background and precedes Contact. It shows up to
three recent published posts with View more linking to `/blog/`. The homepage
Blog menu item targets `/#blog`; writing pages target `/blog/`. Scroll highlighting
follows the actual menu-linked sections. Preserve mobile and keyboard navigation.

Authoring uses one top-level flat `posts/` folder. Markdown front matter accepts
title, date, owner, tags, description and draft; title and a publication date are
required. Owner defaults to Vijayasankar Ravindran, description falls back to an
excerpt, and extra YAML is retained. Legacy pubDate/categories remain accepted.
Drafts stay out of routes, homepage previews, the archive, tags, and RSS.

Preserve Genesis verbatim, its original publication date, tags, feed address,
and historical links. Keep the debt calculator standalone. Hosting targets
GitHub Pages, with no server, database, or external CMS required.
